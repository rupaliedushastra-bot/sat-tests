### Question 1
**Topic:** `Slope & Inclination`  

**Question:**
> What is the slope of a line that is equally inclined to the coordinate axes?

**Options:**
- **A)** $0$
- **B)** $1$
- **C)** $-1$
- **D)** $\pm 1$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) $\pm 1$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. A line equally inclined to the coordinate axes makes an angle of either $45^\circ$ or $135^\circ$ with the positive $x$-axis.
- For $\theta = 45^\circ$: Slope $m = \tan(45^\circ) = 1$.
- For $\theta = 135^\circ$: Slope $m = \tan(135^\circ) = -1$.

Therefore, the possible slopes are $\pm 1$ (Choice D).

### Question 2
**Topic:** `Slope & Inclination`  

**Question:**
> Determine $x$ so that the inclination of the line containing the points $(x, -3)$ and $(2, 5)$ is $135^\circ$.

**Options:**
- **A)** $5$
- **B)** $8$
- **C)** $10$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** $12$

**Correct Answer:** **C) $10$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. The inclination is $\theta = 135^\circ$, so the slope is:
$$m = \tan(135^\circ) = -1$$

Using the slope formula between $(x, -3)$ and $(2, 5)$:
$$m = \frac{5 - (-3)}{2 - x} = \frac{8}{2 - x}$$

Equating to $-1$:
$$\frac{8}{2 - x} = -1 \implies 8 = -(2 - x) = x - 2 \implies x = 8 + 2 = 10$$

### Question 3
**Topic:** `Slope Calculation`  

**Question:**
> The slope of a line joining the points $(7, 3)$ and $(K, 2)$ is $-4$. What is the value of $K$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $\frac{25}{4}$
- **B)** $\frac{29}{4}$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $4$
- **D)** $-\frac{29}{4}$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $\frac{29}{4}$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Using the slope formula $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$:
$$\frac{2 - 3}{K - 7} = -4 \implies \frac{-1}{K - 7} = -4$$

Multiply both sides by $-(K - 7)$:
$$1 = 4(K - 7) = 4K - 28 \implies 4K = 29 \implies K = \frac{29}{4}$$

### Question 4
**Topic:** `Parallel Lines`  

**Question:**
> What is the value of $y$ so that the line through $(3, y)$ and $(2, 7)$ is parallel to the line through $(-1, 4)$ and $(0, 6)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $5$
- **B)** $7$
- **C)** $9$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** $11$

**Correct Answer:** **C) $9$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. Parallel lines have equal slopes.
- Slope of the line through $(-1, 4)$ and $(0, 6)$:
$$m_2 = \frac{6 - 4}{0 - (-1)} = \frac{2}{1} = 2$$

- Slope of the line through $(3, y)$ and $(2, 7)$:
$$m_1 = \frac{y - 7}{3 - 2} = y - 7$$

Setting $m_1 = m_2$:
$$y - 7 = 2 \implies y = 9$$

### Question 5
**Topic:** `Angle of Inclination`  

**Question:**
> What is the angle between the $x$-axis and the line joining the points $(3, -1)$ and $(4, -2)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $45^\circ$
- **B)** $90^\circ$
- **C)** $120^\circ$
- **D)** $135^\circ$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) $135^\circ$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. Find the slope of the line:
$$m = \frac{-2 - (-1)}{4 - 3} = \frac{-1}{1} = -1$$

The angle $\theta$ that the line makes with the positive direction of the $x$-axis satisfies $\tan \theta = m = -1$.
Since the inclination angle $\theta \in [0^\circ, 180^\circ)$:
$$\theta = 180^\circ - 45^\circ = 135^\circ$$

### Question 6
**Topic:** `Parallel & Perpendicular Lines`  

**Question:**
> What is the relationship between the line joining the points $(2, -3)$ and $(-5, 1)$ and the line joining the points $(7, -1)$ and $(0, 3)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** They are parallel. ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **B)** They are perpendicular.
- **C)** They intersect at a $45^\circ$ angle.
- **D)** They are the same line.

**Correct Answer:** **A) They are parallel.**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice A is correct. Calculate the slopes of both lines:
- First line slope: $m_1 = \frac{1 - (-3)}{-5 - 2} = \frac{4}{-7} = -\frac{4}{7}$
- Second line slope: $m_2 = \frac{3 - (-1)}{0 - 7} = \frac{4}{-7} = -\frac{4}{7}$

Since $m_1 = m_2 = -\frac{4}{7}$, the two lines have equal slopes and are therefore parallel.

### Question 7
**Topic:** `Parallel & Perpendicular Lines`  

**Question:**
> What is the relationship between the line joining the points $(2, -5)$ and $(-2, 5)$ and the line joining $(6, 3)$ and $(1, 1)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** They are parallel.
- **B)** They are perpendicular. ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** They intersect at a $60^\circ$ angle.
- **D)** They are the same line.

**Correct Answer:** **B) They are perpendicular.**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Calculate the slopes of both lines:
- First line slope: $m_1 = \frac{5 - (-5)}{-2 - 2} = \frac{10}{-4} = -\frac{5}{2}$
- Second line slope: $m_2 = \frac{1 - 3}{1 - 6} = \frac{-2}{-5} = \frac{2}{5}$

Check the product of their slopes:
$$m_1 \times m_2 = \left(-\frac{5}{2}\right) \times \left(\frac{2}{5}\right) = -1$$

Because the product of their slopes is $-1$, the lines are perpendicular.

### Question 8
**Topic:** `Angle Between Two Lines`  

**Question:**
> What is the tangent of the angle $\theta$ between the lines joining the points $(-1, 2), (3, -5)$ and $(-2, 3), (5, 0)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $\tan \theta = \frac{49}{37}$
- **B)** $\tan \theta = \frac{37}{49}$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $\tan \theta = \frac{12}{37}$
- **D)** $\tan \theta = 1$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $\tan \theta = \frac{37}{49}$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Calculate both slopes:
- $m_1 = \frac{-5 - 2}{3 - (-1)} = \frac{-7}{4} = -\frac{7}{4}$
- $m_2 = \frac{0 - 3}{5 - (-2)} = \frac{-3}{7} = -\frac{3}{7}$

Using the angle between two lines formula $\tan \theta = \left|\frac{m_1 - m_2}{1 + m_1 m_2}\right|$:
$$m_1 - m_2 = -\frac{7}{4} - \left(-\frac{3}{7}\right) = -\frac{7}{4} + \frac{3}{7} = \frac{-49 + 12}{28} = -\frac{37}{28}$$
$$1 + m_1 m_2 = 1 + \left(-\frac{7}{4}\right)\left(-\frac{3}{7}\right) = 1 + \frac{3}{4} = \frac{7}{4} = \frac{49}{28}$$
$$\tan \theta = \left|\frac{-37/28}{49/28}\right| = \frac{37}{49}$$

### Question 9
**Topic:** `Perpendicular Lines`  

**Question:**
> If the line through the points $(-2, 6)$ and $(4, 8)$ is perpendicular to the line through the points $(8, 12)$ and $(x, 24)$, what is the value of $x$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $2$
- **B)** $4$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $6$
- **D)** $-4$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $4$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Find the slope of the first line:
$$m_1 = \frac{8 - 6}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$$

For the two lines to be perpendicular, the slope of the second line must be the negative reciprocal:
$$m_2 = -\frac{1}{m_1} = -3$$

Using the slope formula for the second line:
$$\frac{24 - 12}{x - 8} = -3 \implies \frac{12}{x - 8} = -3 \implies x - 8 = -4 \implies x = 4$$

### Question 10
**Topic:** `Geometric Figures & Slopes`  

**Question:**
> The points $(1, 2)$, $(4, 5)$ and $(6, 3)$ represent the vertices of which type of polygon?

**Options:**
- **A)** Scalene Triangle
- **B)** Equilateral Triangle
- **C)** Right Triangle ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** Isosceles Triangle

**Correct Answer:** **C) Right Triangle**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. Let the vertices be $A(1, 2)$, $B(4, 5)$, and $C(6, 3)$.
Calculate slopes of sides:
- Slope of $AB = \frac{5 - 2}{4 - 1} = \frac{3}{3} = 1$
- Slope of $BC = \frac{3 - 5}{6 - 4} = \frac{-2}{2} = -1$

Since $m_{AB} \times m_{BC} = (1)(-1) = -1$, side $AB$ is perpendicular to side $BC$ (angle $B = 90^\circ$).
Alternatively, by distances:
- $AB^2 = (4-1)^2 + (5-2)^2 = 9 + 9 = 18$
- $BC^2 = (6-4)^2 + (3-5)^2 = 4 + 4 = 8$
- $AC^2 = (6-1)^2 + (3-2)^2 = 25 + 1 = 26$

Since $AB^2 + BC^2 = 18 + 8 = 26 = AC^2$, the triangle is a Right Triangle.

### Question 11
**Topic:** `Collinearity of Points`  

**Question:**
> Which of the following statements is true about the points $P(6, -1)$, $Q(5, 0)$ and $R(2, 3)$?

**Options:**
- **A)** They form a right triangle.
- **B)** They are collinear. ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** They form an equilateral triangle.
- **D)** They are the vertices of a parabola.

**Correct Answer:** **B) They are collinear.**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Three points are collinear if the slope between any two pairs is identical:
- Slope $m_{PQ} = \frac{0 - (-1)}{5 - 6} = \frac{1}{-1} = -1$
- Slope $m_{QR} = \frac{3 - 0}{2 - 5} = \frac{3}{-3} = -1$

Since $m_{PQ} = m_{QR} = -1$ and both line segments share the common point $Q$, the points $P, Q,$ and $R$ lie on the same straight line (they are collinear).

### Question 12
**Topic:** `Collinearity of Points`  

**Question:**
> For what value of $x$ are the points $(x, -1)$, $(2, 1)$ and $(4, 5)$ collinear?

**Options:**
- **A)** $0$
- **B)** $1$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $2$
- **D)** $3$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $1$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Collinear points have equal slopes between every pair:
- Slope between $(2, 1)$ and $(4, 5)$:
$$m = \frac{5 - 1}{4 - 2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$$

- Slope between $(x, -1)$ and $(2, 1)$:
$$\frac{1 - (-1)}{2 - x} = \frac{2}{2 - x}$$

Equating to $2$:
$$\frac{2}{2 - x} = 2 \implies 2 - x = 1 \implies x = 1$$

### Question 13
**Topic:** `Quadrilaterals in Coordinate Plane`  

**Question:**
> The points $(-2, -1)$, $(4, 0)$, $(3, 3)$ and $(-3, 2)$ are the vertices of a:

**Options:**
- **A)** Kite
- **B)** Trapezoid
- **C)** Parallelogram ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** Regular Hexagon

**Correct Answer:** **C) Parallelogram**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. Let the vertices be $A(-2, -1), B(4, 0), C(3, 3), D(-3, 2)$.
Calculate the slopes of all four sides:
- Slope $AB = \frac{0 - (-1)}{4 - (-2)} = \frac{1}{6}$
- Slope $CD = \frac{2 - 3}{-3 - 3} = \frac{-1}{-6} = \frac{1}{6} \implies AB \parallel CD$
- Slope $BC = \frac{3 - 0}{3 - 4} = \frac{3}{-1} = -3$
- Slope $DA = \frac{-1 - 2}{-2 - (-3)} = \frac{-3}{1} = -3 \implies BC \parallel DA$

Since both pairs of opposite sides are parallel, the quadrilateral is a Parallelogram.

### Question 14
**Topic:** `Quadrilaterals in Coordinate Plane`  

**Question:**
> The points $P(2, -2)$, $Q(8, 4)$, $R(5, 7)$ and $S(-1, 1)$ are the vertices of a:

**Options:**
- **A)** Rhombus
- **B)** Trapezoid
- **C)** Rectangle ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** Kite

**Correct Answer:** **C) Rectangle**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. Calculate slopes of all four sides:
- Slope $PQ = \frac{4 - (-2)}{8 - 2} = \frac{6}{6} = 1$
- Slope $RS = \frac{1 - 7}{-1 - 5} = \frac{-6}{-6} = 1 \implies PQ \parallel RS$
- Slope $QR = \frac{7 - 4}{5 - 8} = \frac{3}{-3} = -1$
- Slope $SP = \frac{-2 - 1}{2 - (-1)} = \frac{-3}{3} = -1 \implies QR \parallel SP$

Adjacent sides have slopes $1$ and $-1$, whose product is $1 \times (-1) = -1$ (all angles are $90^\circ$).
Lengths of adjacent sides:
- $PQ = \sqrt{(8-2)^2 + (4-(-2))^2} = \sqrt{36 + 36} = \sqrt{72} = 6\sqrt{2}$
- $QR = \sqrt{(5-8)^2 + (7-4)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 9} = \sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$

Since opposite sides are parallel, all interior angles are right angles, and adjacent sides are unequal, $PQRS$ is a Rectangle.

### Question 15
**Topic:** `Varignon's Theorem & Midpoints`  

**Question:**
> A quadrilateral has vertices at the points $(-4, 2)$, $(2, 6)$, $(8, 5)$ and $(9, -7)$. The midpoints of the sides of this quadrilateral form a:

**Options:**
- **A)** Rectangle
- **B)** Rhombus
- **C)** Square
- **D)** Parallelogram ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) Parallelogram**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. According to Varignon's Theorem, the polygon formed by joining the midpoints of the sides of ANY quadrilateral (convex or concave) is ALWAYS a Parallelogram.

Verification by midpoints:
- $M_1 = \left(\frac{-4+2}{2}, \frac{2+6}{2}\right) = (-1, 4)$
- $M_2 = \left(\frac{2+8}{2}, \frac{6+5}{2}\right) = (5, 5.5)$
- $M_3 = \left(\frac{8+9}{2}, \frac{5-7}{2}\right) = (8.5, -1)$
- $M_4 = \left(\frac{9-4}{2}, \frac{-7+2}{2}\right) = (2.5, -2.5)$

Slopes:
- Slope $M_1 M_2 = \frac{5.5 - 4}{5 - (-1)} = \frac{1.5}{6} = \frac{1}{4}$
- Slope $M_4 M_3 = \frac{-1 - (-2.5)}{8.5 - 2.5} = \frac{1.5}{6} = \frac{1}{4} \implies M_1 M_2 \parallel M_4 M_3$
- Slope $M_2 M_3 = \frac{-1 - 5.5}{8.5 - 5} = \frac{-6.5}{3.5} = -\frac{13}{7}$
- Slope $M_1 M_4 = \frac{-2.5 - 4}{2.5 - (-1)} = \frac{-6.5}{3.5} = -\frac{13}{7} \implies M_2 M_3 \parallel M_1 M_4$

Hence, the figure is a Parallelogram.

### Question 16
**Topic:** `Angle Between Two Lines`  

**Question:**
> The slope of a line is three times the slope of another line. If the tangent of the angle between them is $\frac{1}{2}$, what are the possible slopes of the lines?

**Options:**
- **A)** $\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}$ or $2, 6$
- **B)** $\frac{1}{3}, 1$ or $1, 3$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $\frac{1}{4}, \frac{3}{4}$ or $2, 3$
- **D)** $1, 3$ only

**Correct Answer:** **B) $\frac{1}{3}, 1$ or $1, 3$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Let the slopes of the two lines be $m$ and $3m$.
Using the angle formula $\tan \theta = \left|\frac{m_2 - m_1}{1 + m_1 m_2}\right|$:
$$\tan \theta = \left|\frac{3m - m}{1 + (3m)(m)}\right| = \left|\frac{2m}{1 + 3m^2}\right| = \frac{1}{2}$$

Case 1: $\frac{2m}{1 + 3m^2} = \frac{1}{2}$
$$1 + 3m^2 = 4m \implies 3m^2 - 4m + 1 = 0 \implies (3m - 1)(m - 1) = 0$$
- If $m = \frac{1}{3}$, the slopes are $m = \frac{1}{3}$ and $3m = 1$.
- If $m = 1$, the slopes are $m = 1$ and $3m = 3$.

Thus, the possible pairs of slopes are $\frac{1}{3}, 1$ or $1, 3$ (Choice B).

### Question 17
**Topic:** `Angle Between Two Lines`  

**Question:**
> If the angle between two lines is $\frac{\pi}{4}$ ($45^\circ$) and the slope of one of the lines is $\frac{1}{2}$, what is the possible slope of the other line?

**Options:**
- **A)** $2$ or $-\frac{1}{2}$
- **B)** $3$ or $-\frac{1}{3}$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $1$ or $-1$
- **D)** $4$ or $-\frac{1}{4}$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $3$ or $-\frac{1}{3}$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. We know $\theta = \frac{\pi}{4} \implies \tan \theta = \tan(45^\circ) = 1$. Let $m_1 = \frac{1}{2}$ and the other slope be $m$.
$$\left|\frac{m - 1/2}{1 + (1/2)m}\right| = 1 \implies \left|\frac{2m - 1}{2 + m}\right| = 1$$

- Case 1: $\frac{2m - 1}{2 + m} = 1 \implies 2m - 1 = 2 + m \implies m = 3$
- Case 2: $\frac{2m - 1}{2 + m} = -1 \implies 2m - 1 = -2 - m \implies 3m = -1 \implies m = -\frac{1}{3}$

Therefore, the possible slopes are $3$ or $-\frac{1}{3}$ (Choice B).

### Question 18
**Topic:** `Angle with Coordinate Axes`  

**Question:**
> What is the acute angle between the $y$-axis and the line joining the points $(4, 3\sqrt{3})$ and $(3, 2\sqrt{3})$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $30^\circ$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **B)** $45^\circ$
- **C)** $60^\circ$
- **D)** $90^\circ$

**Correct Answer:** **A) $30^\circ$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice A is correct. Calculate the slope of the line:
$$m = \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 3\sqrt{3}}{3 - 4} = \frac{-\sqrt{3}}{-1} = \sqrt{3}$$

The angle $\theta$ that the line makes with the positive $x$-axis is:
$$\theta = \tan^{-1}(\sqrt{3}) = 60^\circ$$

Because the $y$-axis is perpendicular to the $x$-axis ($90^\circ$), the angle between this line and the $y$-axis is:
$$\text{Acute Angle with } y\text{-axis} = 90^\circ - 60^\circ = 30^\circ$$
