### Question 7 (Module 1 · Q7)

**Question:**
> Clara bought 8 dining chairs that were each the same price. She used a coupon for $\$50$ off the entire purchase. The cost for the entire purchase after using the coupon was $\$110$. What was the original price, in dollars, for 1 chair?

8c - 50 = 110
Add 50 to both sides:

Divide both sides by 8:

Therefore, the original price of 1 chair was $20.

> 
> The given equation describes the relationship between the number of cats, $c$, and the number of dogs, $d$, that can be cared for at an animal shelter on a given day. If the shelter cares for 10 dogs on a given day, how many cats can it care for on this day?

3.5c + 7(10) = 105

3.5c = 35
Divide both sides by 3.5:
c = 35 / 3.5 = 10.
Therefore, the shelter can care for 10 cats on that day.

- Choice A (5) gives 3.5(5) + 70 = 17.5 + 70 = 87.5 ≠ 105.
- Choice C (20) gives 3.5(20) + 70 = 70 + 70 = 140 ≠ 105.
- Choice D (35) is the value of 3.5c before dividing by 3.5.

ΔL = (10/3) × ΔW.

ΔL = (10/3) × 6 = 20 units.
Therefore, the length must increase by 20 units.

### Question 23 (Module 1 · Q23)
**Topic:** `Polynomial Factoring & Divisibility`  

**Question:**
> The expression $5x^2 + cx - 60$, where $c$ is a constant, can be rewritten as $(mx + p)(x + q)$, where $m$, $p$, and $q$ are integer constants. Which of the following must be an integer?

**Options:**
- **A)** $\frac{c}{m}$
- **B)** $\frac{c}{p}$
- **C)** $\frac{60}{m}$
- **D)** $\frac{60}{q}$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) $\frac{60}{q}$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. Expanding the factored form (mx + p)(x + q) gives mx² + (mq + p)x + pq. Equating coefficients with 5x² + cx - 60:
1) m = 5
2) pq = -60
3) c = mq + p = 5q + p.
Since p and q are integers and their product is -60, q must be an integer divisor of -60. Therefore, -60 / q = p is an integer, which implies that 60 / q is always an integer.

Distractor Explanations:
- Choice A (c/m = (5q + p)/5 = q + p/5) is not necessarily an integer because p is not necessarily a multiple of 5 (e.g., if p = 3, q = -20).
- Choice B (c/p) is not necessarily an integer.
- Choice C (60/m = 12) is a constant, but Choice D is the essential algebraic divisibility condition defining the factor q.

x = -b / (2a) = -(-19) / (2 × 2) = 19 / 4 = 4.75.

Choice A is correct. A solution to a system of three linear equations is a point (x, y) that lies on all three lines simultaneously. Writing each equation in slope-intercept form:
1) x + 3y = 6 ⇒ y = -1/3 x + 2
2) 2x + 6y = 24 ⇒ y = -1/3 x + 4
3) x + 3y = -12 ⇒ y = -1/3 x - 4

### Question 53 (Module 2 · Q26)
**Topic:** `3D Geometry & Surface Area`  

**Question:**
> Two identical rectangular prisms each have a height of $80\text{ centimeters (cm)}$. The base of each prism is a square, and the surface area of each prism is $M\text{ cm}^2$. If the prisms are glued together along a square base, the resulting prism has a surface area of $\frac{41}{21}M\text{ cm}^2$. What is the side length, in $\text{cm}$, of each square base?

**Options:**
- **A)** 4
- **B)** 8 ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** 10
- **D)** 16

**Correct Answer:** **B) 8**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Let $s$ be the side length in centimeters of the square base, and let the height be $h = 80\text{ cm}$.

Each individual prism has 2 square bases of area $s^2$ and 4 rectangular side faces of area $80s$. Thus, the surface area $M$ of one prism is:
$$M = 2s^2 + 4(80)s = 2s^2 + 320s$$

When the two prisms are glued together along one square base, the two contacting square bases are hidden inside the combined solid. The surface area of the resulting prism is:
$$\text{Combined Surface Area} = 2M - 2s^2 = 2(2s^2 + 320s) - 2s^2 = 2s^2 + 640s$$

We are given that this combined surface area is equal to $\frac{41}{21}M$:
$$2s^2 + 640s = \frac{41}{21}(2s^2 + 320s)$$

Multiply both sides by 21:
$$21(2s^2 + 640s) = 41(2s^2 + 320s)$$
$$42s^2 + 13,440s = 82s^2 + 13,120s$$

Rearrange and collect like terms:
$$(82 - 42)s^2 = (13,440 - 13,120)s$$
$$40s^2 = 320s$$

Since the side length $s > 0$, divide both sides by $40s$:
$$s = \frac{320}{40} = 8\text{ cm}$$

Therefore, the side length of each square base is $8\text{ cm}$ (Choice B).

Distractor Explanations:
- Choices A ($4$), C ($10$), and D ($16$) do not satisfy the surface area ratio of $\frac{41}{21}$.

### Question 54 (Module 2 · Q27)
**Topic:** `Percent Increase & Proportions`  

**Question:**
> $320$ is $p\%$ greater than $40$. What is the value of $p$?

**Options:**
- **A)** 280
- **B)** 700 ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** 750
- **D)** 800

**Correct Answer:** **B) 700**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. '320 is p% greater than 40' means the amount of increase over 40 is (p/100) × 40. Setting up the equation:
320 = 40 + (p/100) × 40
Subtract 40 from both sides:
280 = (p/100) × 40
Divide both sides by 40:
280 / 40 = p / 100
7 = p / 100
p = 7 × 100 = 700.
Check: 40 increased by 700% is 40 + 7(40) = 40 + 280 = 320.

Distractor Explanations:
- Choice A (280) is the absolute amount of increase (320 - 40), not the percentage.
- Choice C (750) is an arithmetic miscalculation.
- Choice D (800) is (320 / 40) × 100% = 800% (the total percentage that 320 is OF 40, not GREATER than 40).
