### Question 1 · `[EASY]`
**Topic:** `Exponential Initial Value`  

**Question:**
> The function $f(x) = 450(1.08)^x$ models the number of employees at a company $x$ years after it was founded. How many employees did the company have when it was founded?

**Options:**
- **A)** $1.08$
- **B)** $8$
- **C)** $108$
- **D)** $450$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) $450$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. When the company was founded, $x = 0$ years have passed.
Substitute $x = 0$ into the function:
$$f(0) = 450(1.08)^0 = 450(1) = 450$$
Thus, the company had $450$ employees when founded (represented by the initial value $a$ in $f(x) = ab^x$).

### Question 2 · `[EASY]`
**Topic:** `Exponential Decay Modeling`  

**Question:**
> A new computer was purchased for $\$1,200$. It depreciates in value by $20\%$ each year. Which equation models the value, $V(t)$, in dollars, of the computer $t$ years after it was purchased?

**Options:**
- **A)** $V(t) = 1200(0.20)^t$
- **B)** $V(t) = 1200(0.80)^t$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $V(t) = 1200(1.20)^t$
- **D)** $V(t) = 1200 - 20t$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $V(t) = 1200(0.80)^t$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. An exponential decay model has the form $V(t) = P(1 - r)^t$, where $P$ is the initial price and $r$ is the annual decay rate.
Here $P = 1200$ and $r = 0.20$:
$$V(t) = 1200(1 - 0.20)^t = 1200(0.80)^t$$

### Question 4 · `[EASY]`
**Topic:** `Exponential Decay Identification`  

**Question:**
> Which of the following functions represents an exponential decay model?

**Options:**
- **A)** $y = 100(1.01)^x$
- **B)** $y = 50(0.95)^x$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $y = 0.5(1.5)^x$
- **D)** $y = 20(2)^x$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $y = 50(0.95)^x$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. An exponential function $y = ab^x$ (with $a > 0$) represents exponential decay if and only if $0 < b < 1$.
In Choice B, $b = 0.95 < 1$, which represents a $5\%$ decrease per period. All other options have $b > 1$, which represent exponential growth.

### Question 8 · `[MODERATE]`
**Topic:** `Finding Exponential Parameters`  

**Question:**
> An exponential function is given by $y = ab^x$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. If the graph of the function passes through the points $(0, 5)$ and $(2, 45)$, what is the value of $b$ assuming $b > 0$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $3$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **B)** $5$
- **C)** $9$
- **D)** $40$

**Correct Answer:** **A) $3$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice A is correct. Substitute $(0, 5)$ into $y = ab^x$:
$$5 = a b^0 = a(1) \implies a = 5$$

Now substitute $(2, 45)$ and $a = 5$:
$$45 = 5 b^2 \implies b^2 = \frac{45}{5} = 9$$

Since $b > 0$, $b = \sqrt{9} = 3$.

### Question 10 · `[MODERATE]`
**Topic:** `Equivalent Exponential Forms`  

**Question:**
> A city's population $P$ is modeled by the equation $P(t) = 15000(1.2)^{t/3}$, where $t$ is in years. The equation can be rewritten in the form $P(t) = 15000(b)^t$. Which of the following is equivalent to $b$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $1.2$
- **B)** $0.4$
- **C)** $(1.2)^3$
- **D)** $(1.2)^{1/3}$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*

**Correct Answer:** **D) $(1.2)^{1/3}$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice D is correct. Using exponent rules $(x^{mn} = (x^m)^n)$:
$$P(t) = 15000(1.2)^{t/3} = 15000\left((1.2)^{1/3}\right)^t$$
Comparing with $P(t) = 15000(b)^t$, we find $b = (1.2)^{1/3}$.

### Question 11 · `[MODERATE]`
**Topic:** `Ratio of Exponential Models`  

**Question:**
> Two investment accounts are opened at the same time. Account A starts with $\$100$ and grows by $10\%$ annually. Account B starts with $\$200$ and grows by $5\%$ annually. Which expression represents the ratio of the balance in Account A to the balance in Account B after $t$ years?

**Options:**
- **A)** $0.5(1.05)^t$
- **B)** $0.5\left(\frac{1.10}{1.05}\right)^t$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $2\left(\frac{1.10}{1.05}\right)^t$
- **D)** $0.5(0.05)^t$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $0.5\left(\frac{1.10}{1.05}\right)^t$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. The balance functions are:
- Account A: $A(t) = 100(1 + 0.10)^t = 100(1.10)^t$
- Account B: $B(t) = 200(1 + 0.05)^t = 200(1.05)^t$

The ratio $\frac{A(t)}{B(t)}$ is:
$$\frac{A(t)}{B(t)} = \frac{100(1.10)^t}{200(1.05)^t} = \frac{100}{200}\left(\frac{1.10}{1.05}\right)^t = 0.5\left(\frac{1.10}{1.05}\right)^t$$

**Topic:** `Annual Growth Rate Conversion`  

**Question:**
> An asset's value is determined by the function $f(t) = 1000(1.44)^{t/2}$, where $t$ is the number of years. This function shows that the asset's value increases by what percentage each year?

**Options:**
- **A)** $20\%$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **B)** $22\%$
- **C)** $44\%$
- **D)** $72\%$

**Correct Answer:** **A) $20\%$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice A is correct. Rewrite the function with an exponent of $t$:
$$f(t) = 1000(1.44)^{t/2} = 1000\left((1.44)^{1/2}\right)^t = 1000(\sqrt{1.44})^t = 1000(1.20)^t$$
Since $1.20 = 1 + 0.20 = 1 + 20\%$, the asset increases by $20\%$ each year.

### Question 15 · `[SUPER TOUGHEST]`
**Topic:** `Solving Exponential Rates`  

**Question:**
> A population grows by $r\%$ every year. Exactly $10\text{ years}$ later, the population is exactly $3$ times its initial size. Which of the following equations correctly expresses $r$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $r = 30$
- **B)** $r = 100(3^{1/10} - 1)$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $r = 100(3^{10} - 1)$
- **D)** $r = 100(1 - 3^{1/10})$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $r = 100(3^{1/10} - 1)$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Let the initial population be $P_0$. The population after 10 years is:
$$P(10) = P_0\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{10} = 3P_0$$

Divide by $P_0$:
$$\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^{10} = 3 \implies 1 + \frac{r}{100} = 3^{1/10} \implies \frac{r}{100} = 3^{1/10} - 1 \implies r = 100(3^{1/10} - 1)$$

### Question 18 · `[SUPER TOUGHEST]`
**Topic:** `Exponent Properties`  

**Question:**
> Which of the following expressions is equivalent to the exponential function $C(t) = 100(1.05)^{t+2}$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $C(t) = 102(1.05)^t$
- **B)** $C(t) = 110.25(1.05)^t$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $C(t) = 100(1.05)^t + 100(1.05)^2$
- **D)** $C(t) = 200(1.05)^t$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $C(t) = 110.25(1.05)^t$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Use the exponent product rule $a^{x+y} = a^x \cdot a^y$:
$$C(t) = 100(1.05)^{t+2} = 100 \cdot (1.05)^2 \cdot (1.05)^t$$
Since $(1.05)^2 = 1.1025$:
$$C(t) = 100 \cdot 1.1025 \cdot (1.05)^t = 110.25(1.05)^t$$

### Question 19 · `[SUPER TOUGHEST]`
**Topic:** `Exponential Decay Modeling`  

**Question:**
> The population of a specific town decreases exponentially. If the population was $40,000$ in $2010$ and $32,400$ in $2012$, which of the following models the population $P$, $t$ years after $2010$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $P(t) = 40000(0.81)^t$
- **B)** $P(t) = 40000(0.9)^t$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **C)** $P(t) = 40000(0.9)^{t/2}$
- **D)** $P(t) = 40000 - 3800t$

**Correct Answer:** **B) $P(t) = 40000(0.9)^t$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice B is correct. Let $t = 0$ represent the year 2010, so $P(0) = 40000$. In 2012, $t = 2$ and $P(2) = 32400$.
Using $P(t) = 40000(b)^t$:
$$32400 = 40000(b^2) \implies b^2 = \frac{32400}{40000} = \frac{324}{400} = 0.81 \implies b = \sqrt{0.81} = 0.9$$
Thus, the population model is $P(t) = 40000(0.9)^t$.

### Question 20 · `[SUPER TOUGHEST]`
**Topic:** `Multi-year Rate Balancing`  

**Question:**
> A stock drops in value by $20\%$ in its first year. For the next two years, it grows by a constant annual rate $r$ (expressed as a decimal) until it reaches its original starting value. What is the value of $r$?

**Options:**
- **A)** $r = 0.10$
- **B)** $r = \sqrt{1.2} - 1$
- **C)** $r = \sqrt{1.25} - 1$ ✅ *(Correct Answer)*
- **D)** $r = 0.125$

**Correct Answer:** **C) $r = \sqrt{1.25} - 1$**

**Detailed Solution & Explanation:**
Choice C is correct. Let $V_0$ be the original starting value.
- After Year 1 (20% drop): $V_1 = V_0(1 - 0.20) = 0.80 V_0$
- After Year 3 (grows at rate $r$ for 2 years): $V_3 = 0.80 V_0(1 + r)^2$

Set $V_3 = V_0$:
$$0.80 V_0(1 + r)^2 = V_0 \implies 0.80(1 + r)^2 = 1 \implies (1 + r)^2 = \frac{1}{0.80} = 1.25$$
$$1 + r = \sqrt{1.25} \implies r = \sqrt{1.25} - 1$$
(Note: $\sqrt{1.25} \approx 1.118 \implies r \approx 11.8\%$ per year).
